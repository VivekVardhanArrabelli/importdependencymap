Two axes, not one magic score

Separate what matters to India from what you can execute.

Every market is read on two independent axes: national importance from trade evidence, and personal feasibility from the mandate you declare. Gross imports do not by themselves prove dependence, a domestic capability gap, factory feasibility, or an attractive investment.

Trade evidence

Annual import value, baseline change, monthly history, partner mix, and data period come from trade records.

B

Axis one

National importance

Import-value scale and momentum identify large or accelerating pools where domestic capability could matter. This axis does not claim that you should enter.

  • Missing facts are omitted, never converted to zero
  • Sector groups come from HS chapters, not substring guesses
  • Structural commodities remain visible but are flagged
  • Controlled goods stay out of default founder results
  • Aircraft and vessels carry a strategic-asset gate
  • Every card explains why it surfaced
C

Axis two

Personal feasibility

Role, cheque, capital-stack access, time to a paid pilot, entry model, strengths, buyer access, and sector familiarity shape the route you could test.

  • — reviewed business-entry routes
  • — catalogue markets with any unsourced CAPEX estimate
  • Cheque × capital stack creates modeled launch envelopes, not project quotations
  • Unsourced project CAPEX is excluded from ranking because project CAPEX coverage is not yet systematic
  • Technical feasibility and unit economics are not yet verified
  • No self-reported progress score or readiness badge is created

Data roadmap

Better data exists—and should be added deliberately.

Current automated source UN Comtrade Reliable international HS6 coverage and partner comparisons. Preferred India source DGCI&S TradeStat Official India data with deeper HS8 detail; use official exports, not brittle scraping. Official enrichment Trade Intelligence & Analytics Useful validation and India-specific trade context. Domestic capability baseline MoSPI Annual Survey of Industries Factory output, inputs, fixed assets, and employment; requires a careful NIC-to-HS concordance. Production reality check MoSPI Index of Industrial Production Monthly output for 1,042 industrial items; useful after a careful product concordance. Cluster signal Udyam MSME open data District-level enterprise density for supplier and location screening. Compliance gate BIS compulsory certification Current product-specific QCO and certification obligations must be checked before launch. Approval discovery National Single Window System Use the official portal to identify central and state approvals for the actual project.
